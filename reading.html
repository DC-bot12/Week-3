In the opinionated article, With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster, by Nesrine Malik it talks about the rise of artificially created content. Content that lacks any blood, sweat, and tears but instead uses electricity, land, and water. The article discusses how the use of AI has turned it into the easiest propaganda tool for the United States government. It brings up the countless situations from the White House from them using artificial intelligence as propaganda for the arrests ICE has been conducting, ridiculing several prominent democratic figures, and responding to other countries making fun of the United States. The article continues to discuss how its being weaponised by being hard to fact-check or even detest when the video or photo in question has been doctored. With the technology getting more and more sophisticated, the usage has also increased but not for good. Alongside with propaganda, people have created a lot of deepfakes and media to push their own narratives. To compound the issue is that many social media companies do not care as the content is engaging for the audience which increases their profit from advertisement revenue. Referencing back to the title of the article, the more nonresponding the social media companies are, the more these posts and content will eventually be baked into our lives. The worry in the article is that these lazy artificially created content will look too realistic and trick everyday users. In my opinion, the usage of Artificial intelligence slop is a problem and will be problematic for the future to come. While there is easy methods to detect AI use, these methods surely will disappear and too uncommon for the average person to be concerned with. Many people often create opinions on situations not based on facts but based on emotions so if they see a post with AI to exaggerate the problem to higher proportions than it actually is, it can cause people to misinterpret the situation at hand. More importantly, these social media companies should be held accountable for not moderating or protecting its users on if posts are artificial or not. Considering that these apps connect billions of people worldwide, they should be responsible on what gets posted or displayed on their app as the posts can be seem by people who are vulnerable. In addition, the AI slop will not stop unfortunately as it is too useful and beneficial for the company to allow its use. As previously mentioned, the content is highly engaging. Many people either cannot tell the difference or is unaware of the use of AI which for the company means more people on the app to scroll and communicate with others about the post(s). To conclude, while I believe that these social media giants should be held accountable, the AI slop will not stop.
